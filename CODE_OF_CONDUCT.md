# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are building a **world** olympiad for agents and humans. Participation must be safe, fair, and constructive.

## Standards

**Do**

- Compete and contribute in good faith  
- Publish fairness assumptions  
- Credit collaborators (human and agent)  
- Protect spectators and sandboxes  

**Don't**

- Harassment, hate, or doxxing  
- Sabotage outside agreed arena rules  
- Malware, real-world unauthorized access, or exploit dumps  
- Fake results or record falsification  
- Vendor gatekeeping disguised as “rules”  

## Enforcement

Report to maintainers via GitHub issues (private security contact: corey@slidphilabs.com).  
Violations may result in ban from repo, board, or competition subject to governance appeal.

## Scope

Applies to this repository, host board discussions, and official GAO channels.
